### <mark>**Kinematics**</mark> - motion with no cause of it
**Vector** - quantities that have a direction
**Scalars** - quantities that don't have direction

***Cartesian coordinate system***
- One dimensional - consists of a straight line (one axis - x)
- Two dimensional - consists of two perpendicular lines (two axis - x,y)
- Three dimensional - consists of three perpendicular lines (three axis - x,y,z) 
 
 ***Sylindric coordinate system***
 - Two dimensional - r vector, φ angle
 - Three dimensional - r vector, φ angle and z (the same as Cartesian)
   `x=|r|cosφ`
   `y=|r|sinφ`
   ***Spherical coordinate system***
 - Three dimensional - r vector, φ angle, θ angle
 - ![](attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260917183147.png)
`x=|r|cosθsinφ`
`y=|r|sinθsinφ`
`z=|r|cosθ`

<mark>**Linear movement**</mark>

i, j, k - are the standard unit vectors pointing along the x, y, and z axes of a three-dimensional Cartesian coordinate system 
$\vec{r} = x\vec{i} + y\vec{j} + z\vec{k}$ 
$r^2 = x^2 + y^2 + z^2$ 
$r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$ 

Average speed - scalar value
$$\frac{S}{t} = \frac{distace}{time} = v$$
Average velocity - vector value
$$\frac{d\vec{r}}{dt} = r'(t) =  \vec{v}$$
Acceleration - vector value
$$\frac{\Delta{\vec{v}}}{\Delta{t}} = \frac{d\vec{v}}{dt} = \frac{d^2\vec{r}}{dt^2} = \ddot{r}(t) = \vec{a} \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \frac{dvx}{dt} = \frac{d^2x}{dt^2} = \ddot{x}(t) = a_{x}$$
